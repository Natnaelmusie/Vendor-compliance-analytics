## **Vendor Compliance Insights (Power BI)**

The pipeline takes messy data entered by the inbound team and feeds it into an insightful Power BI dashboard, helping make better decisions by identifying the worst offenders; the worst times, days, and months; and whether a trend emerges.

### Overview

Across 30 vendors and 1,839 deliveries, the overall on-time rate is 80.2%, with 267 deliveries non-compliant.

Most vendors perform well, and non-compliance is concentrated in a small number of them, so the focus is on pinpointing those specific vendors rather than judging the group as a whole.

From the *Vendor Volume by On-Time Rate* table, we can see that vendors with higher delivery volumes have some of the lowest on-time rates — though this could simply be that a greater number of deliveries increases the chances of various compliance issues arising.

When we look at the vendors with the lowest delivery volumes, they tend to have the highest on-time rates, as fewer deliveries mean fewer opportunities for failure. This isn't always the case, however: the vendor with the lowest on-time rate also has one of the lowest delivery volumes.

### Vendors & Timing

Across the 1,839 deliveries, the non-compliant deliveries are separated into three categories based on severity.

##### **Category 1 — "Late under 1 hour": 108 deliveries.**

These deliveries fall past the grace period (assumed to be 30 minutes for all deliveries). They aren't late enough to be the real problem-makers, but they're still worth flagging, as adjusting the booked time or delivery day may reduce these numbers.

##### **Category 2 — "Late 1 to 24 hours": 95 deliveries.**

Higher severity. Top 5 vendors in this category:

* ZAK — 21 deliveries  
* NRL — 14 deliveries  
* XPR — 10 deliveries  
* HCN — 8 deliveries  
* NHP — 8 deliveries

These vendors have the most deliveries that are late by more than an hour, or even delayed by a full day. This causes logistics problems and disrupts transport planning: if customers need bulk orders and these vendors are late, we disappoint customers — which likely leads to a loss of trust and future income. A useful approach would be to identify the best time and date for these vendors, and possibly move them to the open-slot booking type to allow more time for delivery *(worth testing: do open-slot bookings improve on-time rates for unreliable vendors?)*, so the warehouse can receive the items and put them into stock, ready to be picked and shipped when needed.

##### **Category 3 — "Late over 24 hours": 40 deliveries.**

These deliveries are delayed by more than a day, which compounds the problems seen in Category 2:

Delayed deliveries → stock issues in the warehouse → more reversal orders → more unhappy customers.

They also make storage planning difficult. If a delivery doesn't arrive as scheduled, the assigned storage space could have been used for something else; if that space is reallocated and the delivery then arrives two days later, there's nowhere to put it. This may be manageable in quieter quarters, but during peak quarters it becomes a real problem.

Top vendors in Category 3:

* NRL — 12 deliveries  
* ZAK — 7 deliveries  
* HCN — 4 deliveries  
* XPR — 4 deliveries  
* NHP — 3 deliveries

These 5 vendors account for 75% of Category 3, making it clear which vendors should be targeted. They also appear in all three categories, which further justifies having direct conversations or applying fines.

More stats:

- Worst time to receive, regardless of data,  is always an unscheduled delivery.  
- Worst fixed time is equally 13:30 pm and 11:30 am   
- Worst day with highest late rate is Tuesday   
- Second worst day is Wednesday.  
- Lowest rate is on a Thursday.

##### **The Trends** 

Importance of these trend charts:

Q4 (October–December) is the industry's peak period for order volume — Black Friday and Christmas. The data shows the late rate climbing sharply across exactly those months (14.37% in October to 25.63% in December), meaning compliance is at its weakest precisely when demand is highest. The most costly time for it to slip.
